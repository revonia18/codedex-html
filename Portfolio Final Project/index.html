A Little About Me My full name is Revonia Nosimilo Mguni, and I'm a girl from Joburg who loves creating, exploring, and trying new things. My curiosity eventually led me into technology, where I discovered how much I enjoy turning ideas into something real through code and design. But I'm not just interested in the technical side — I also love marketing, social media, communications, and event planning. I'm naturally organised and enjoy bringing ideas together, planning behind the scenes, and helping projects come to life.
